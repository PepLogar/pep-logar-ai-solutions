Frequently asked questions

What matters before installing.

01When does the 14-day trial start?

After the first successful PrestaShop connection, not when the extension is installed.

02What remains after the trial?

The permanent free mode still identifies the product or combination and opens its exact Back Office page.

03Is the licence a subscription?

No. Shop and Pro are perpetual, one-time-payment licences.

04Does it install a module?

No. It is a Chrome and Edge extension and does not modify PrestaShop code.

05Where is the AI key stored?

In the local storage of the browser profile. Requests go directly to the selected provider.

06Can I edit products with variants?

Yes. The extension detects the selected combination and works with the corresponding data.
